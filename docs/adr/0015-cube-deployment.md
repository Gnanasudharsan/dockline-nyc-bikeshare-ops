# 0015. Cube deployment: Cube Store, pre-aggregations, read-only access

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-07

## Context
Cube needs a cache/queue and somewhere to keep pre-aggregations in production mode.
Live panels refresh every 10 seconds; history charts scan millions of 5-minute rows.

## Decision
- Run **Cube Store** as its own container (single node). Its amd64 and arm64 builds have
  different tags, so the tag is `CUBESTORE_IMAGE_TAG` (arm64 by default for the Mac and
  the Oracle Ampere VM; CI overrides it to amd64).
- Refresh keys follow the data: `fleet_status` (live view) every 10 s with no
  pre-aggregation; `station_status` every 5 minutes; hourly and daily cubes hourly.
- Pre-aggregations:
  - `station_status.by_borough_5min`: network and borough totals per 5-minute bucket,
    day partitions, incremental with a 6-hour update window, 35-day build range (same
    as the fact's retention).
  - `station_hourly.by_borough_hour`: hourly minutes and flows by borough, hour of day,
    peak flag, weather, and day type; month partitions.
  Point-in-time and ratio measures are built from additive sums so rollups stay exact.
- Cube connects as `dockline_reader` (migration 0010): `SELECT` on every warehouse
  schema, nothing else, 60-second statement timeout. Its password is generated by
  `make env` and applied by the migrate job on every run.

## Consequences
- History charts read small rollups; live panels hit a ~200 ms view at most every 10 s.
- The serving layer cannot modify the warehouse even if a query is crafted against it.
