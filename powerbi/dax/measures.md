# DAX measures

Generated by `powerbi/build_pbip.py` — do not edit by hand.

Each measure has the same name as the Cube measure in the cube its table mirrors
(`fleet_status`, `station_hourly`, `chronic_stations`, `weather`), so Power BI and
Cube give the same answer for the same question. Definitions: `docs/metrics.md`.

## fleet_status (Cube: `fleet_status`)

Live state of every listed station. Mirrors the Cube cube fleet_status.

| Measure | Folder | Format | DAX | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bikes_available` | Live\Availability | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(SUM(fleet_status[station_bikes_available]), fleet_status[is_in_service] = TRUE())` | Bikes a rider could rent right now (in-service stations; includes e-bikes). |
| `ebikes_available` | Live\Availability | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(SUM(fleet_status[station_ebikes_available]), fleet_status[is_in_service] = TRUE())` | E-bikes a rider could rent right now. |
| `docks_available` | Live\Availability | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(SUM(fleet_status[station_docks_available]), fleet_status[is_in_service] = TRUE())` | Empty docks a rider could return to right now. |
| `usable_slots` (hidden helper) | Live\Availability | `#,0` | `[bikes_available] + [docks_available]` | Bikes plus free docks at in-service stations (helper). |
| `fill_ratio` | Live\Availability | `0.0%` | `DIVIDE([bikes_available], [usable_slots])` | bikes_available / (bikes_available + docks_available). |
| `imbalance_index` | Live\Availability | `0.000` | `[fill_ratio] - 0.5` | fill_ratio minus the 0.5 target. Negative = starving, positive = flooding. |
| `stations` | Live\Station counts | `#,0` | `COUNTROWS(fleet_status)` | Stations listed in the feed. |
| `stations_in_service` | Live\Station counts | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fleet_status), fleet_status[is_in_service] = TRUE())` | Stations reporting, installed, and renting or returning. |
| `empty_stations` | Live\Station counts | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fleet_status), fleet_status[is_in_service] = TRUE(), fleet_status[station_bikes_available] = 0)` | In-service stations with no bike to rent right now. |
| `full_stations` | Live\Station counts | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fleet_status), fleet_status[is_in_service] = TRUE(), fleet_status[station_docks_available] = 0)` | In-service stations with no free dock right now. |
| `starving_stations` | Live\Station counts | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fleet_status), fleet_status[imbalance_state] = "starving")` | Stations in the starving state right now. |
| `balanced_stations` | Live\Station counts | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fleet_status), fleet_status[imbalance_state] = "balanced")` | Stations in the balanced state right now. |
| `flooding_stations` | Live\Station counts | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fleet_status), fleet_status[imbalance_state] = "flooding")` | Stations in the flooding state right now. |
| `offline_stations` | Live\Station counts | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fleet_status), fleet_status[imbalance_state] = "offline")` | Stations in the offline state right now. |
| `rebalance_need_bikes` | Live\Rebalancing | `#,0` | `SUM(fleet_status[bikes_to_move])` | Bikes to add plus bikes to remove to bring starving and flooding stations to half full. |
| `longest_empty_minutes` | Live\Rebalancing | `0` | `MAX(fleet_status[minutes_empty])` | Longest current empty spell among the stations in context (minutes). |
| `data_freshness_seconds` | Live\Data quality | `#,0` | `DATEDIFF(MAX(fleet_status[last_reported_at_utc]), UTCNOW(), SECOND)` | Seconds since the newest station report (now − latest last_reported). |

## station_hourly (Cube: `station_hourly`)

Station activity per clock hour. Mirrors the Cube cube station_hourly.

| Measure | Folder | Format | DAX | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `in_service_station_minutes` | History\Minutes | `#,0` | `SUM(station_hourly[in_service_minutes])` | Station-minutes in service. |
| `empty_station_minutes` | History\Minutes | `#,0` | `SUM(station_hourly[empty_minutes])` | Minutes stations had no bike to rent while renting. |
| `full_station_minutes` | History\Minutes | `#,0` | `SUM(station_hourly[full_minutes])` | Minutes stations had no free dock while returning. |
| `available_station_minutes` | History\Minutes | `#,0` | `SUM(station_hourly[available_minutes])` | In-service minutes with at least one bike and one dock. |
| `offline_station_minutes` | History\Minutes | `#,0` | `SUM(station_hourly[offline_minutes])` | Station-minutes not in service. |
| `availability_rate` | History\Rates | `0.0%` | `DIVIDE([available_station_minutes], [in_service_station_minutes])` | Share of in-service station-minutes with at least one bike and one dock. |
| `empty_share` | History\Rates | `0.0%` | `DIVIDE([empty_station_minutes], [in_service_station_minutes])` | Share of in-service station-minutes with no bike. |
| `full_share` | History\Rates | `0.0%` | `DIVIDE([full_station_minutes], [in_service_station_minutes])` | Share of in-service station-minutes with no free dock. |
| `net_flow` | History\Flow | `#,0` | `SUM(station_hourly[net_flow_bikes])` | Change in bikes on hand. Includes rebalancing trucks; not trips. |
| `bikes_added` | History\Flow | `#,0` | `SUM(station_hourly[added_bikes])` | Sum of positive 5-minute changes. |
| `bikes_removed` | History\Flow | `#,0` | `SUM(station_hourly[removed_bikes])` | Sum of negative 5-minute changes (as a positive number). |
| `station_hours` | History\Minutes | `#,0` | `COUNTROWS(station_hourly)` | Station-hours observed. |

## chronic_stations (Cube: `chronic_stations`)

Weekday-peak verdict per station over the last 14 days. Mirrors chronic_stations.

| Measure | Folder | Format | DAX | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `chronic_problem_stations` | Chronic | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(chronic_stations), chronic_stations[chronic_problem_station] = TRUE())` | Stations empty or full > 25% of in-service weekday-peak minutes (≥ 10 peak hours observed). |
| `stations_rated` | Chronic | `#,0` | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(chronic_stations), chronic_stations[chronic_status] <> "insufficient_data")` | Stations with enough peak data for a verdict. |
